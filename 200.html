ℹ️ About CIVIL ESTIMATION A1

CE-A1

CIVIL ESTIMATION A1

2D Plan Upload & AI Takeoff System

CIVIL ESTIMATION A1 is a civil engineering estimation software designed to provide fast, code-compliant preliminary quantity takeoffs and Bill of Quantities (BOQ) from 2D architectural drawings.

Code & Standard References:
  • IS 456:2000: Code of Practice for Plain and Reinforced Concrete.
  • IS 1200 (Parts 1–28): Method of Measurement of Building and Civil Engineering Works.
  • IS 2212: Code of Practice for Brickwork.
  • CPWD DSR 2023–26: Delhi Schedule of Rates & Analysis of Rates for building civil works.
